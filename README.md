# Decision Wheel

Can't decide? Let the wheel choose for you!

Add your options, spin the wheel, and get a ranking.

## Links

- **Open the app:** https://othman00011.github.io/decision-wheel/
- **Code:** https://github.com/othman00011/decision-wheel

## How to use

1. Type an option and press **Add**.
2. Add as many options as you want.
3. Press **Start**.
4. Press **Spin**.
5. The wheel picks rank 1. Spin again for rank 2, and so on.

You can also choose **Quick pick** to get only one winner.

## Features

- Spinning wheel
- Full ranking or one winner
- Ready-made templates
- Saves your history
- Copy your result
- Sound and vibration
- Light and dark mode
- Works on phone, tablet and computer
- Works offline

## Install on your phone

- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app**.
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Run on your computer

1. Download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
